Send links and notes to your own devices

Found something on your laptop that you'll want on your phone? Send it with Passel, and it's waiting on your other devices straight away. Sign in with the same Google account everywhere; what you send is kept under your account and shown only to you.

Open Passel

  1. Sign in

    Use your Google account in the Chrome extension, the Android app and on the web.

  2. Send

    Send the page you're on, a link or a note from whichever device you have to hand.

  3. Pick it up

    It shows up on your other devices at once, newest at the top, until you delete it.
